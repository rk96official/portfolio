Accurate calculation of the Earth Pressure using numerical integration

LaGuardia Community College • December 2017

Research Abstract

This research calculated the pressure inside the Earth as a function of distance from the center, utilizing two different Earth models to demonstrate the importance of realistic density profiles in geophysical calculations. The study achieved accurate pressure calculations through advanced numerical integration techniques.

Key Findings

  • Calculated Earth's center pressure at approximately 364 GPa using the Preliminary Reference Earth Model (PREM)
  • Demonstrated that uniform density models underestimate center pressure by a factor of 2 (172 GPa vs 364 GPa)
  • Computed Earth's rotational inertia as 8.02×10³⁷ kg·m², showing 20% reduction from naive uniform distribution calculations
  • Validated the critical importance of realistic density profiles for accurate geophysical modeling
  • Showed that mass concentration at Earth's center significantly affects rotational properties

Methodology

  • Implemented Simpson's 1/3 rule for numerical integration of complex density functions
  • Applied the Preliminary Reference Earth Model (PREM) for realistic density distribution
  • Compared results between uniform density and realistic density models
  • Used Python with SciPy for mathematical computations and numerical analysis
  • Integrated pressure equations from Earth's surface to center using variable density profiles
  • Calculated mass enclosed as a function of radial distance for accurate force calculations

Mathematical Framework

  • Derived pressure equations from hydrostatic equilibrium principles
  • Used gravitational force balance with pressure gradient forces
  • Applied spherical coordinate system for Earth's layered structure
  • Implemented numerical integration with appropriate step sizes for accuracy

Scientific Contributions

  • Demonstrated practical application of numerical integration in geophysics
  • Provided educational framework for understanding Earth's internal structure
  • Showed quantitative impact of density variations on pressure calculations
  • Created computational model for Earth's internal pressure distribution

Technical Tools & Methods

Python SciPy Numerical Integration Scientific Computing Geophysical Modeling LaTeX

Research Impact

This work demonstrated the critical importance of using realistic Earth models in geophysical calculations and provided a computational framework that could be applied to other planetary bodies. The study showed how advanced numerical methods can provide accurate solutions to complex geophysical problems.
